AVAHAN 2027 rulebook

Common rules

These apply to every sport. Sport-specific rules are in the full PDF.

  1. 01
    Reporting time

    Teams report 30 minutes before the scheduled match time.

  2. 02
    One team per sport

    A player can only play for a single team in each sport.

  3. 03
    ID proof

    Every player's college ID is checked at the match. Without a valid ID, bring this year's fee receipt and a photo ID.

  4. 04
    Zero violence

    Any violence, or verbal or physical abuse of referees, event heads or other teams, disqualifies the whole squad.

  5. 05
    Undertaking and shoes

    An undertaking letter and proper sports shoes are compulsory for all participants.

  6. 06
    Final decision

    The event head's and referee's decision is final in every match.
